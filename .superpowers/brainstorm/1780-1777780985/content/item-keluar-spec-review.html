Key Design Decisions

Unit Price — entered manually per item (not auto-filled from catalog)
Bill Split — partial allowed, sum of PIC amounts may be less than totalLoss
Stock on PT Approval — delta applied: qty↓ restores stock · qty↑ decreases further · removed product restores · added product decreases
Version types — only "original" and "approved" (no "instant")
